## 0. What this document is, and what it is not

This dossier puts two certification gates on the record so an authorised operator can decide what to
do about them. **It changes nothing.** No threshold in `certification_policy.py` has been edited
(v1.2.0, `policy_sha256 = 4e050ad00ff6dcf8c00661e8976486025109a63f6144a9afe8e3e60a2387f664`,
unchanged). No candidate has been promoted. `promotion_permitted` remains `false`.

**The decision below is the operator's. This document prepares it; it does not pre-empt it.**

Both gates currently FAIL. Neither failure is a bug to be fixed by more code from here. They are two
different kinds of hard limit, and they need two different kinds of decision:

- **G18** may be *unsatisfiable by construction* — a Rule 14 candidate. It needs a policy decision.
- **G16** is *research-blocked with a measured, unexplained result*. It needs a disposition
  statement, not a policy change.

## 2. G18 — `market_baseline`: FAIL, and possibly unsatisfiable by construction

### 2.1 What the gate requires

`PROMOTION_GATES["market_baseline"]` requires the candidate to beat the de-vigged market RPS **in
every one of six leagues**. It is a point comparison with no notion of sampling error.

### 2.2 What was measured

`docs/DEBT.md` item 62 (2026-09-06, PR #156) ran a **paired block bootstrap**, 10,000 replicates,
block size 10, on `apex_v5_66` versus the de-vigged market. Pairing matters: both heads stacked into
one array so one set of resampled block indices applies to both. The paired interval measured ~34×
narrower than an unpaired equivalent — an unpaired test could not have resolved the effect at all.

| League | Candidate RPS | Market RPS | Diff | 95% CI | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| BUNDESLIGA | 0.1968 | 0.1908 | +0.0059 | [+0.0004, +0.0106] | **market beats candidate** |
| EPL | 0.2049 | 0.2054 | **−0.0005** | **[−0.0029, +0.0028]** | indistinguishable |
| EREDIVISIE | 0.1988 | 0.1969 | +0.0019 | [−0.0009, +0.0049] | indistinguishable |
| LA_LIGA | 0.1964 | 0.1963 | +0.0001 | [−0.0038, +0.0034] | indistinguishable |
| LIGUE_1 | 0.2034 | 0.1991 | +0.0043 | [−0.0014, +0.0095] | indistinguishable |
| SERIE_A | 0.1994 | 0.1971 | +0.0022 | [−0.0010, +0.0056] | indistinguishable |

(RPS lower is better; positive diff = candidate worse.)

**0 of 6 leagues have a CI excluding zero in the candidate's favour.** The same run scored the
**serving incumbent** `v5_phase7` against the market on the same persisted arrays: **0 of 6** there
too, with SERIE_A significantly worse.

The single interval that *does* exclude zero (Bundesliga) runs **against** the model.

### 2.3 Three reasons the 1/6 point-estimate "win" is not evidence

1. **The interval swamps the effect.** EPL's CI half-width is 5.7× the 0.0005 point estimate the
   earlier carve-out proposal was built on.
2. **It was selected after the fact.** EPL was chosen as best-of-six *after* observing all six.
   Under the null that the model merely equals the market, ~3/6 would come out ahead by chance. So
   1/6 is evidence **against** the model, not for it.
3. **It is worse than what already serves.** In EPL, `apex_v5_66` scores 0.2049 against the serving
   incumbent's own 0.2036 — 0.0013 *worse* than production, roughly 3× the claimed market edge.

### 2.4 The structural reason

DID §54.2: *the raw de-vigged market outscored both fitted arms on 2 of 3 folds.* A model trained on
market-derived probabilities does not reliably improve on them at this feature count.

Independently corroborated by `docs/DEBT.md` item 43, from a different metric and a different
question: the de-vigged market's own Brier score (0.5787, sum-over-classes) fails a 0.220 bar by
2.6×. The market is not highly accurate in absolute terms — and nothing in this repository beats it
anyway.

### 2.5 Is it a Rule 14 defect?

**Not established.** Unlike G1's hard 62.35% ceiling, no proof exists that *no* feature set can beat
the market. What is established is that four independent measurements (items 43, 50, 58/59, 62) have
landed on the same answer from four angles.

⚠️ **Explicitly NOT done: the gate was not loosened.** Changing
`{"leagues_beating_market": "all"}` after observing that it is what blocks promotion is precisely
what APEX §23 prohibits, and confidence that a change is correct does not make it safe to make
without authorisation.

### 2.6 Options for the operator

| # | Option | What it means | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Amend to a CI-based rule** | Replace the all-leagues point comparison with "≥N leagues whose paired 95% CI excludes zero in the candidate's favour, Bonferroni-corrected". | Statistically the most defensible. **Today's candidate still fails it (0/6).** Passes nothing now; makes the gate meaningful later. |
| **B** | **Restrict scope to leagues with demonstrated edge** | Gate only on leagues where an edge has been measured. | **Currently vacuous** — the qualifying set is empty. Becomes a live option only after a real edge exists somewhere. |
| **C** | **Accept as serving-only under `PROMOTE_RESEARCH_ONLY`** | Record that this generation serves forecasts and is not certified to beat the market; the gate stands unchanged and unmet. | Honest and immediately actionable. Changes no threshold. Consistent with the ADR-0011 posture already live. |

**Prepared recommendation (the operator decides):** **C now, A as a follow-up.** C matches the
deployed reality without touching a threshold. A is worth doing on its own merits — a point
comparison at these margins is not a sound instrument regardless of which candidate it judges — but
it must be adopted because it is *better statistics*, not because it is what would let something
through. Adopting A while it still fails 0/6 is the cleanest way to establish that motive.

`scripts/bootstrap_market_edge_ci.py` is the reusable instrument for Option A and is the standard any
future market-beating claim should be held to.

## 3. G16 — `uncertainty`: FAIL, research-blocked, ADR-governed

### 3.1 Current live effect

`MODEL_UNCERTAINTY_UNAVAILABLE` is emitted unconditionally on `/full-analysis`, forcing `partial`,
forcing `stake_permitted: false`. Verified live 2026-09-20 on a real Serie A fixture
(`fd-558591`): 5 critical gaps including `MODEL_UNCERTAINTY_UNAVAILABLE`, `stake_permitted: false`,
`odds_edge: null`.

⚠️ **Consequence worth stating plainly: certifying the model would NOT by itself open
`/full-analysis` staking.** That surface is blocked independently by this gap. The two are separate
locks on the same door.

### 3.2 What is and is not on the table

Three paths are already closed and must not be reopened:

- ❌ **Do not add `torch`. Do not train a BNN.** Item 42 is CLOSED/superseded; its "Option 1" is
  obsolete.
- ❌ **Probability-derived epistemic uncertainty is permanently rejected** — `1 − max(p)`,
  `entropy(p)`, `1 − confidence`, `1 − Σp²`. Self-referential; carries no information the prediction
  does not already have (ADR-0009).
- ✅ **Ensemble dispersion is the sanctioned source**, and it is built, real, and **certified on 5 of
  6 criteria**.

### 3.3 The single open question

`error_association` fails, and the direction of the failure is the finding: the **highest**-epistemic
quartile scores **better** than the lowest, across all 5 scoreable leagues. Five hypotheses have been
examined and four ruled out — including the calculation-bug hypothesis (`ensemble_uncertainty.py`
audited directly, PR #122) and in-bag contamination and aleatoric confounding (PR #124). The cause
remains genuinely unexplained (`docs/DEBT.md` item 50, open).

Two `xfail` markers pin this and **print the live measurement rather than suppressing it**. They have
not been touched.

### 3.4 What mitigates it today

The ADR-0011 circuit breaker (`backend/src/services/risk_guard.py`) suppresses staking for any
fixture at or below its league's measured epistemic p25 — the concentrated danger zone. Measured
per-league hit-rate deltas inside that zone:

| League | Threshold | Hit-rate delta inside the zone |
|---|---:|---|
| EPL | 0.0788 | −7.3 pp |
| BUNDESLIGA | 0.0879 | −15.3 pp |
| LIGUE_1 | 0.0859 | −6.5 pp |
| LA_LIGA | 0.0776 | −0.7 pp (flat) |
| SERIE_A | 0.0848 | **+5.5 pp (reversed)** |

⚠️ **These five are not one finding with five confirmations.** SERIE_A reverses and LA_LIGA is flat.
All five are suppressed regardless, because a false positive costs a missed opportunity while a false
negative costs a user's money, and this generation has no demonstrated edge to forgo.

⚠️ **The breaker mitigates the CONSEQUENCE. It does not resolve the CAUSE.** Presenting it as a fix
for G16 would be the error this dossier exists to prevent.

### 3.5 Options for the operator

| # | Option | What it means | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Accept as a documented limitation of this generation** | Record: ensemble-dispersion uncertainty is certified on 5 of 6 criteria, with a measured and unexplained inversion on the sixth; G16 is an accepted limitation; the breaker mitigates the consequence. | No threshold changes. `stake_permitted` stays `false` on `/full-analysis`. Honest; immediately actionable. |
| **B** | **Fund hypothesis 5+** | Continue root-causing the inversion with a real measurement. | Open-ended. No estimate is offered, because none is evidenced. |
| **C** | **Reclassify `MODEL_UNCERTAINTY_UNAVAILABLE` to advisory** | Would unblock `/full-analysis` staking. | ⚠️ **Loosens a staking safety gate after observing that it is what blocks a desired result.** Squarely APEX §23. Listed only for completeness; **not recommended**, and it would stake precisely where the uncertainty signal is known to be inverted. |

**Prepared recommendation (the operator decides):** **A, with B as unscheduled follow-up.** A states
the truth without moving a gate. C is listed because a complete dossier should not hide the option
the operator might otherwise ask about — but it is the one action this dossier actively argues
against.

## 4. Precise scope of what the operator is being asked to accept

If the operator accepts the prepared recommendations (G18 → C, G16 → A), the accepted risk is:

1. **The serving generation has no demonstrated edge over the de-vigged market in any league** —
   0/6 by paired 95% CI, for both the candidate and the incumbent. Stakes published under ADR-0011
   are therefore not backed by a measured market edge.
2. **The model's own uncertainty signal is inverted** in a way that remains unexplained. It is least
   reliable where its ensemble agrees most. The circuit breaker declines the measured danger zone;
   it does not repair the signal.
3. **Calibration is measurably poor on real evidence** — adaptive-confidence ECE **0.0977** against a
   0.03 policy ceiling, measured 2026-09-20 on 87 real settled predictions across all 6 served
   leagues (`docs/DEBT.md` item 113). Live `/full-analysis` confirms `calibration_method: "raw"`,
   `calibration_applied: false`.
4. **These are standing conditions, not a transient state.** Nothing scheduled resolves any of them.

**What is NOT being asked for, and must not be inferred from acceptance:**

- ❌ Not a certification. `certification_state` must remain `OPERATOR_OVERRIDE_UNCERTIFIED`.
- ❌ Not a change to any threshold in `certification_policy.py`.
- ❌ Not an extension of ADR-0011's blast radius. `staking_authorization()` has exactly one
  production consumer (`services/upcoming_match_service.py`). Every other surface asks
  `active_generation_is_certified()`. Swapping one for the other anywhere else silently widens a
  Class C authorization and requires amending ADR-0011 first.

## 5. Reversibility

Everything here is reversible and none of it has been actioned.

- The ADR-0011 override reverses by deleting the `operator_override` block from
  `backend/models/active_generation.json` and setting `certification_state` back to `"UNVERIFIED"`.
- No gate threshold has been edited, so there is nothing to revert in `certification_policy.py`.
- A disposition statement (G16 option A, G18 option C) is a documentation act and reverses by
  amendment.
